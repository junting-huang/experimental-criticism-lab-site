How do media technologies reshape reading and interpretation? What can computational systems understand about literature? What forms of criticism are needed in data-driven culture? How do machines turn texts into data? What remains illegible, excessive, or resistant? What can criticism build, test, or unsettle? How does reading change across language, scale, and medium?
