## Join the Lab

The Experimental Criticism Lab welcomes undergraduate and graduate students throughout the academic year. There's no application to join: participation begins simply by showing up—attending events, joining meetings, and contributing to shared projects.

Each semester opens with an information session, followed by an orientation to current projects and regular working meetings focused on research, writing, and project development. Students who sustain their engagement across a semester may take on formal roles as research fellows or associates, with opportunities for co-authorship and, where funding allows, compensation. Graduate students can also formalize their participation through a one-credit independent study.

The best first step is to attend an information session or email directly.
