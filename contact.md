## Join the Lab

The lab is open to all interested students during the academic year—undergraduate and graduate alike. There is no application to join at the outset; participation begins simply by showing up and contributing.

Each semester opens with an information session, followed by orientation to current projects and then regular working meetings focused on research, writing, and development. Students who demonstrate sustained engagement over a semester may take on formal roles as research fellows or research associates, with opportunities for co-authorship and, where funding allows, financial compensation. Graduate students may also formalize their participation through a one-credit independent study.

The best first step is to attend an information session or reach out directly.
