<p class="contact-lede">The Experimental Criticism Lab welcomes undergraduate and graduate students throughout the academic year.</p>

<section class="contact-section">
  <h2>How to Participate</h2>
  <div class="contact-copy">
    <p>There's no application to join: participation begins simply by showing up—attending events, joining meetings, and contributing to shared projects.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="contact-section">
  <h2>Opportunities</h2>
  <div class="contact-copy">
    <p>Each semester opens with an information session, followed by an orientation to current projects and regular working meetings focused on research, writing, and project development.</p>
    <p>Students who sustain their engagement across a semester may take on formal roles as research fellows or associates, with opportunities for co-authorship and, where funding allows, compensation. Graduate students can also formalize their participation through a one-credit independent study.</p>
  </div>
</section>
